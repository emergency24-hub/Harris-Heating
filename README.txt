HARRIS HEATING WEBSITE

OPEN
Extract the whole ZIP, then open index.html in your browser.
Keep the files and assets folder together. No build or server is required.
Call and email links open your device's calling or email application.

EDIT
Edit company details, services and FAQs in the BUSINESS block at the top of
script.js. Save and refresh. Keep service IDs unchanged; they match page names.
Change appearance and animations in style.css. Images and icons are in assets.
HTML also includes fallback content. When changing business details, update
matching HTML text, page titles, descriptions and canonical URLs as appropriate.

PUBLISH
Upload the contents of this folder to a static web host. README.txt can be
omitted from the upload. Canonical URLs, robots.txt and sitemap.xml use
harrisheatingltd.co.uk. The package does not deploy or change your domain.
